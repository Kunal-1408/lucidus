# 03: Country of residence, Home state, Citizenship and Residence status

**What to build:** A Student anywhere can say where they live and who they are. The form asks Country → state/region (ISO codes, regions of that country only) → Home state (abroad only) → Residence status (abroad only) → Citizenship (Indian citizen, OCI card holder, another nationality with its country), and shows category only to Indian citizens and OCI holders. Extraction records these as Criteria and the Matcher judges them. Added beside the old domicile and school-state fields, which keep working until ticket 09.

See spec: "Profile", "Which questions appear" (other conditional questions), "Criteria and matching", glossary terms Country of residence, Home state, Citizenship, Residence status.

## Comments

**2026-10-24 (agent):** Built on `feat/abroad-03-residence-citizenship`, then merged with ticket 04 from `integrate/abroad-wave-1`; every box is met.

Profile (both copies): `countryOfResidence` (ISO 3166-1 alpha-2), `region` (ISO 3166-2), `homeState` (the 36 Indian state slugs), `residenceStatus` (`citizen` | `permanent_resident` | `visa`), `citizenship` (`indian` | `oci` | `other`) and `citizenshipCountry`. The country and region lists are generated from Debian's iso-codes data (`iso_3166-1.json`, `iso_3166-2.json`, top-level subdivisions only, common names where Debian has one, India's labels taken from `STATES`) into `frontend/src/app/countries.ts` and `backend/src/match/countries.ts` (`COUNTRIES`, `REGIONS`); the contract test checks the two copies are equal. `STATE_REGIONS` (both copies, also checked) maps each Indian state to its ISO region (current codes: `IN-OD`, `IN-TS`, `IN-UK`, `IN-CG`). The page's initial bundle is about 440 kB with the lists in, under the 500 kB warning; if 07's subject list pushes it over, load `countries.ts` with a dynamic `import()`.

Frontend rules (`profile-rules.ts`): `unaskedForResidence(profile)` hides region without a country, Home state and Residence status unless the country is given and not `IN`, Citizenship country unless `other`, and category for `other` only (an unanswered Citizenship still asks category, the module's convention). `withResidenceClears` does the non-visibility clears: a country change drops region, Home state and Residence status, and a region not in the country's `REGIONS` is never kept. `homeStateOf` reads the Home state from the region in India; `profileForMatch` uses it as the school state fallback. Migration is the `withHomeState` step of 04's `migrateSavedProfile`: `domicileState` becomes `IN` plus its region (dropped if a country is already given or it is no state). The form follows the spec order (Class, country, region, Home state, Residence status, Citizenship and its country, board, boards upcoming and Board marks, gender, category, income, field of study, destination); the domicile question is gone, "State your school is in" stays under "More about you" until 09.

Backend validation (`match/profile-input.ts`): `residenceAnswers` checks the codes and lists (reasons like `countryOfResidence must be an ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 country code`), `residenceContradictions` mirrors the rules (`region must be a region of countryOfResidence`, `homeState is only asked when countryOfResidence is not IN`, `category is only asked when citizenship is indian or oci`, ...).

Matching (`match/matcher.ts`, `derivedAnswer`): the Home state of a Student in India is their region; `homeState` and the old `domicileState` answer each other, so old Profiles and old Criteria keep matching until 09; an Indian citizen counts as citizenship country `IN`, an OCI card holder's is unknown. All new Criterion fields are `in` and never changeable; `citizenship` changed from `eq` to `in` (stored `eq` Criteria still compare).

Extraction (`ingestion/extraction-rules.ts`): domicile sentences now give `homeState`; `domicileState` joined 04's `LEGACY_FIELDS`, so neither the rules, the LLM nor JSON sources produce it. Citizenship reads NRIs as `indian` and OCI/PIO as `oci`. The residence block (`RESIDENCE_RULES`) reads places a sentence names, longest name first, regions before countries ("New South Wales" is not Wales, "Northern Ireland" not Ireland): `countryOfResidence` from a residence cue (residents of, residing in, living in) naming a country other than India; `region` the same for AE, AU, CA, GB and US regions (with English aliases for the emirates); `residenceStatus` from "permanent residents" with a country (plus `citizen` when citizens are named too); `citizenshipCountry` from citizens of a country with no residence cue. "Residents of India" deliberately gives nothing, so Indian sources are unchanged. Codes are upper-cased in `normaliseValue`. The LLM field guide describes each new field; region codes are never listed in full. e2e: `backend/test/residence-citizenship.e2e-spec.ts` over `fixtures/abroad/*.html`. Indian regression scenarios pass unchanged.

For later tickets: 06 (income abroad) should take the currency default from `countryOfResidence` and key its own "abroad" rules off the same `INDIA` check as `unaskedForResidence`. 07 (destination) can reuse `COUNTRIES`, and its destination-is-residence check compares with `countryOfResidence`; a subject list belongs in its own file so the bundle can be split. 09 should delete `domicileState` (Profile, `FIXED_LISTS`, `CRITERION_FIELDS`, `LEGACY_FIELDS`, the `derivedAnswer` cases, `withHomeState`) and `schoolState` with its form question and the `profileForMatch` fallback, and lapse Overrides naming them.
