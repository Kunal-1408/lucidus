# Scope is school students in Class 9–12; abroad scholarships are undergraduate only

Lucidus sells to schools, so the only Student is a school student in Class 9–12. Abroad scholarships are included only when they fund undergraduate study a Class 11–12 Student could plan for; postgraduate schemes such as Chevening are excluded even though they are well known. This keeps the Profile and the class labels small and the results relevant.

## Amendment: Indian scholarships for entering undergraduate study

Indian scholarships that a Class 12 Student applies for on entering undergraduate study (first-year awards such as Santoor, AICTE Pragati and Saksham, or LIC's General Scholarship) are in scope too, since a Class 12 Student plans for them just as for abroad undergraduate study. The Student is still a school student; scholarships for students already partway through a degree, and postgraduate schemes, stay out.

## Amendment: Students living outside India

A large share of Lucidus's Students live outside India: Indian citizens abroad, OCI card holders and other nationalities, at CBSE schools in the Gulf as well as on IB, Cambridge, UK and US curricula. A Student may now live in any country, and scholarships open to school students living abroad (schemes for NRI, PIO and OCI students such as SPDC, and awards run in the Student's country of residence) are in scope and added to the Watchlist. The Student is still a school student in Class 9–12 or the equivalent grade. Results and income are never converted between scales or currencies: a criterion on a different scale or currency than the Student's answer is unknown, never a guessed pass.
